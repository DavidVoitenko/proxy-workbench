FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about proxies and privacy

What is a proxy and why do I need it? ▾

A proxy acts as an intermediary between your device and the internet. Websites see the proxy's IP address instead of your real IP, allowing you to bypass regional restrictions, access blocked services, and protect your privacy.

What is the difference between HTTP and SOCKS5? ▾

HTTP/HTTPS proxies are designed for web browsers and websites. SOCKS5 is universal: it works with any application, handles DNS resolution through the proxy, supports UDP, and is ideal for Telegram, voice calls, and games.

Why do free proxies stop working after some time? ▾

Public proxies are hosted on servers worldwide and can be overloaded or restarted. Proxy Workbench includes an automatic Keep-Fresh (Watch) mode that continually re-checks working proxies every few minutes in the background, keeping your pool 100% active!

How does the Local Rotating Gateway (127.0.0.1:8899) work? ▾

Instead of copying dozens of proxy addresses manually into your apps, you configure only one address: 127.0.0.1:8899. The gateway automatically routes each new connection through the best verified proxy, instantly switching if any proxy drops.

Where is my data stored and is it private? ▾

100% of your data, history, and settings are stored locally on your own computer in the data/ folder. The application is completely offline-first, has no tracking, no cloud telemetry, and sends no personal data anywhere.
